How a reform moves

Every bill in the table is somewhere on this path. The momentum meter roughly tracks how far along — and how fast.

Stage 01

Introduced

A legislator files the bill and it gets a number. Sponsor count starts here.

Stage 02

Committee

Hearings, markups, and amendments. The highest-mortality stage — most bills die here.

Stage 03

Floor

The full chamber debates and votes; then it must clear the second chamber too.

Stage 04

Enacted

Signed by the governor — or approved at the ballot box as a measure.

Stage 05

Implemented

Rule-making, funding, and the first election actually run under the new system.

Methodology & data sources

How this tracker is maintained

Where the rows come from. Bill text, sponsors, and actions are pulled from Open States and individual state legislature feeds, cross-checked against the National Conference of State Legislatures. Ballot-measure status comes from state election divisions and Ballotpedia filings.

Momentum scoring. The 0–100 momentum meter blends sponsor count, committee progress, recent action recency, crossover status, and press signal. Floor-vote odds are analyst estimates, clearly labeled, and never a prediction we stake reporting on.

Cadence & corrections. Rows refresh every Monday at 6am ET; breaking actions are logged within 24 hours. Spot an error? Email the Tracker Desk — we publish corrections.
