The civic glossary

The words the system uses to lose you

Full glossary →

Gerrymandering

Drawing district lines to favor one party or group — "packing" opponents into a few seats or "cracking" them across many.

Cloture

The Senate procedure to end debate and force a vote. It now requires 60 senators — the threshold the filibuster turns on.

Ballot initiative

A law or amendment placed directly on the ballot by citizen petition, bypassing the legislature entirely.

Dark money

Political spending by groups that aren't required to disclose their donors — so you can't see who's paying.

Quorum

The minimum number of members who must be present for a legislature to do business. Denying it can stall a vote.

Standing

Whether a party has the right to bring a lawsuit. Courts often resolve election cases on standing, never reaching the merits.
