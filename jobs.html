How we hire

Transparent, paid, and respectful of your time

Five steps, no surprises. Every skills exercise is paid at our contractor rate, and we tell you where you stand at each stage.

Step 1

Apply

A short application — no cover letter required. Tell us why this work, and show us something you've made.

Step 2

Intro call

A 30-minute conversation with the hiring manager about the role, the mission, and your questions. No trick questions.

Step 3

Skills exercise

A scoped, real-world task that mirrors the job — capped at a few hours and paid at our standard contractor rate.

Step 4

Team interview

Meet the people you'd work with. We walk through your exercise together and talk about how we collaborate.

Step 5

Offer

A written offer with a transparent salary band and references on request. We aim to close out the full process in under three weeks.
