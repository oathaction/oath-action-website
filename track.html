How the dashboard is built

Methodology & sources

Sources. Bill and vote data is drawn from Open States, individual state legislature feeds, and the National Conference of State Legislatures. Money figures come from FollowTheMoney, state campaign-finance portals, and federal disclosure filings. Implementation tracking pulls from agency rule-making dockets and court records.

The momentum index. Each reform is scored 0–100 on a weighted blend of sponsor count, committee progress, crossover status, recent hearings, and press signal. It measures legislative energy, not our endorsement — we are nonpartisan and take no position on any bill.

Cadence. The full dataset refreshes every Monday at 6am ET. Breaking actions — a floor vote, a veto, a signature — are logged within 24 hours and flagged Live on the relevant row.
