Map your issue

Three questions turn an issue into a target

Caring about something isn't a plan. To move a reform, you need to know who decides it, who shapes that decision, and the exact point where outside pressure lands.

①

Who decides

The body with formal authority — a committee chair, an agency director, a county board. Find the seat where the vote or the signature actually happens.

②

Who influences

The donors, staffers, party leaders, and editorial pages whispering before the vote. Influence is quieter than authority — and often louder in effect.

③

Where you push

The one reachable point in the chain where your call, comment, or coalition tips the margin. We mark it red on every systems map.
