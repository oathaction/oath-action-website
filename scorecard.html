Methodology & sources

How this scorecard is built

Every figure on this page is drawn from public records: roll-call votes from the state legislature's journal, committee minutes, official press releases, and campaign-finance disclosures filed with the Secretary of State. We do not estimate, model, or infer positions where a public record does not exist — those appear as "No record."

Neutrality statement. Oath & Action is nonpartisan and takes no position on candidates or parties. Party affiliation is deliberately omitted from this profile. "Reform votes aligned" measures only how often the legislator voted with the specific reforms we track — it is not a measure of quality, ideology, or party.

  • Vote alignment — share of key recorded votes on tracked reforms where the legislator voted with the reform, 2018–2026.
  • Responsiveness index — composite of median reply time, town-hall frequency, and published constituent-service data, normalized 0–100 across the chamber.
  • Funding sectors — standard nonpartisan donor classification applied to itemized disclosure filings.

Found an error? Records change and clerks amend filings. Flag a correction and we will review it against the primary source within five business days.
