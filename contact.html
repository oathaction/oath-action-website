Before you sign up

Common questions

Do I need any experience or a background in politics?

No. Most of our volunteers are curious people with day jobs in something else entirely. We'll match the task to your time and skills, and every role comes with a short playbook so you're never guessing.

How much time does volunteering take?

As little or as much as you want. Some stewards send one message a week; others lead a local coalition. The form lets you set your availability, and we never pressure you to do more.

Is Oath & Action partisan? Will I be asked to back a party?

Never. We're strictly nonpartisan. Our volunteer work is about process reforms — fair maps, transparent money, accessible voting — not candidates or parties. If a role ever feels otherwise, tell us; that's a bug, not a feature.

What happens after I submit the form?

A real person from our community team reads it and replies within two business days with a couple of concrete ways to get started near you. No bots, no spam, and you can step back anytime.
